About

Academic background

I joined the School of Digital Economics at Shanghai University of Finance and Economics as an Assistant Professor in August 2025. I received my Ph.D. in Applied Economics from Beijing Jiaotong University in 2025, where I studied under Professor Zhigang Cao. My doctoral dissertation examines cost allocation and comparative statics in minimum-cost-spanning-tree games.

Research

Research interests

01

Network Games

Strategic interaction on networks, comparative statics, network structure, and cooperative allocation problems.

02

Market Design

Exchange economies, matching, co-ownership, exclusion, and the design of allocation mechanisms.

03

Cooperative Game Theory

Core-based solution concepts, Shapley value, axiomatic analysis, and minimum-cost-spanning-tree games.
